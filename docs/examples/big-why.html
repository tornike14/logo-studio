Big Why · app icon proposal

Every why goes one step deeper.

The orb, resting at the bottom of the question. Drawn after looking at 64 icons from journaling, reflection, habit and AI apps on the US App Store.

  1. Retire the Y. A letter with a full stop is exactly how stoic, a direct competitor with 36,000 ratings, already draws its icon.
  2. Don't ship a plain orb either. Dot, Sesame, Hume, Opal and Meta AI already did, and Siri owns the idea.
  3. Use Depth. Five discs step down to one warm core. It is the only icon in this category you look into.
1 · The field

Six ways the category draws itself

Icons pulled from the App Store for the apps people compare us to, plus the AI assistants a voice app gets lined up against. Sorted by what each icon draws.

Faces and mascots

13

The loudest group. A face promises a character who cares about you. It is warm on day one and childish by month three, and it is the opposite of a quiet tool for adults.

Letters and wordmarks

An initial on a plain ground. Clean, and interchangeable. Our current Y. sits here, beside stoic's S. with the same full stop.

Orbs, dots and rings

11

Where AI voice products went. A plain glowing sphere is already Dot, Sesame, Hume, Opal and Meta AI, before Siri. An orb alone will not be ours.

Sparkles, bursts and knots. They say “this is AI”. Big Why should say what it is for.

Sun, leaf, flame, heart

Voice, books and tools

13

The literal group: a waveform for voice, a book for journaling, a check for done. It describes the feature and says nothing about the reason.

2 · What is missing

Everything sits on the surface

No depth

Every icon is a flat object on a flat ground: a face, a letter, a leaf. None has an inside. Big Why is about what sits under your goals, and that idea is drawn nowhere.

Everyone shouts

Saturated fills and smiling faces compete for the eye. On a busy home screen the calm icon is the one you notice, and it suits a tool people open in a quiet moment.

Nobody draws the reason

Icons draw the feature (a waveform, a book, a check) or a mood (a sun, a heart). Big Why is named after a reason. The icon can draw one.

The current icon's thought is a good one: Why. with a full stop turns the question into an answer. But the grammar of a heavy letter and a dot belongs to stoic, which sits next to us in search results and in every comparison list. Keep the full stop. Lose the letter.

3 · The idea

Draw what the person actually does

They ask why, and then why again. Each answer sits a little below the last one. Under enough of them is something that is theirs and doesn't move.

The orb is the object people touch in the app every day. The icon is the same orb before it wakes, resting at the lowest point, where a ball in a bowl always comes to rest. You don't need to know the story to feel it. You look into the icon and your eye drops to the warm point at the bottom.

The coral core is the full stop from the old Y., kept. The answer was always the dot.

One idea

Depth. Nothing on the icon is decoration. Take any disc away and the idea weakens.

Geometry that settles itself

Every disc shares one axis and shrinks by the same ratio. The only judgment made by eye is how far each step lifts.

One warm point

The core is the only warm colour on the icon. It is the coral the app already uses for people.

Built from layers

iOS 26 builds icons from layers and renders them as glass. Five flat discs are five layers, and the system adds the light.

Right at 29 pixels

It has to read in Settings and Spotlight as well as on the store page. Checked down to 29 px.

6 · Appearances

Light, dark, tinted, clear

iOS 26 shows each icon in default, dark, clear and tinted versions. The mono version is the hard test: if it holds as grey steps and a white point, the glass and tinted versions hold too.

8 · The hand-off

Open the app and the orb rises

The launch screen is the icon. On the first frame the steps open outward and the core lifts from the bottom of the well to the middle of the screen. Icon, launch and home become one object.

9 · Considered and set aside

Three other answers

The plumb line

One line, one weight, true vertical: living by your values. Striking, but it reads as a map pin or a hanging ornament, and the line thins out at small sizes.

The orb on its own

The obvious choice. It is the grammar of AI voice apps now, and at small sizes it is a blurred dot.

Keep the Y.

A clever thought in a borrowed shape. A letter can't hold depth, and the letter-plus-dot shape is stoic's.

To make it real

  • Build the .icon in Icon Composer (Xcode 26): ground, the four rings as one group, the core as its own group, plus a mono version.
  • Replace the app icon, splash and favicon files, and the three Android adaptive layers.
  • Android crops to a circle. The figure is 69% of the canvas, a little over the 66% safe zone, so scale it to 92% on the foreground layer.
  • Run a USPTO search, including nested-circle marks in class 9, before spending on branding.
