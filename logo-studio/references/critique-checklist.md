# Critique checklist

Run this on the size board in every round of gate 5. Be hard on your own work. A round that finds nothing wrong was not a real round.

## Shape

- Does one idea read in under two seconds, without the explanation?
- Does it still read at 40 px? At 29? At 16 for a favicon? Look at the zoomed copies on the board.
- Squint test: blurred, is it still one clear shape, or a grey patch?
- Where shapes meet, is there a knot, a pinch or a sliver? Tangent curves often make dirty points, so move them apart.
- Are thin lines surviving? A line under about 3% of the width disappears at small sizes.
- Does the weight feel balanced, or does it lean or sit too high? Round shapes usually need to sit slightly low.

## Colour and contrast

- Does it work in one colour?
- Does it work reversed, light on dark?
- On a white page, on black, on a busy photo, does the edge hold?
- Are neighbouring tones far enough apart to stay separate when small?
- Do shadows or glows look muddy? Remove them. On iOS 26 the system adds its own light.

## Meaning

- Could this belong to a direct competitor? Hold it against the field sheet.
- Does it accidentally look like something else? Check for a map pin, a target, a chat bubble, anything rude, and how it looks upside down. Say each reading out loud.
- Is it a known logo? Compare it with the closest look-alikes.
- If the brand changed its name, would the mark still make sense?

## Craft

- Are all measurements part of the rule, apart from recorded optical corrections?
- Do the wordmark and the mark share a weight and a feeling? Is the spacing even by eye?
- Is any effect there to hide a weak shape?

## Before moving on

Name the single worst thing in the current best concept, and fix that. Stop only when the remaining problems are ones you would defend to the user.
